Annotation rules

  • Box the entire physical cutter, including carbide body and diamond table.
  • Include worn, chipped, spalled, or partially missing diamond material when the cutter's physical boundary remains identifiable.
  • Annotate every separately visible cutter in the image.
  • Skip an image when no cutter is visible or its physical boundary cannot be identified reliably.
  • Do not box only the diamond table.

Dataset isolation

Class: full_cutter for both views.

Annotations are saved separately under dataset/full_cutter_damage_v1/. No YOLO export is performed from this page.
